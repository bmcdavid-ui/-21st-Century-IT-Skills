> **Goal:** To establish **DAGITAB Martial Arts Academy** as a recognizable academy and turn website visitors into active students through effective promotion, accessible information, and a convenient enrollment process.

**What the website offers**

| Section | Highlights |
|:--|:--|
| **Hero** | "Unleash Your Inner Fire." with Enroll Now and Explore Our Programs buttons |
| **About** | "More than training. It's a way of life." with four core values |
| **Programs** | Taekwondo (kids, teens, adults), Kickboxing, and Kali-Arnis |
| **Why DAGITAB** | Six selling points, from All Ages Welcome to Supportive Community |
| **Gallery** | "Feel the energy. Experience the discipline." |
| **Enrollment** | "Your journey starts here." with an inquiry form |
| **Call to Action** | "Don't just watch. Start training." |

**Brand palette:** `#080808` Deep Black • `#171717` Dark Charcoal • `#D90016` Intense Red • `#FF1E2D` Bright Red • `#FFFFFF` White • `#D1D1D1` Light Gray
