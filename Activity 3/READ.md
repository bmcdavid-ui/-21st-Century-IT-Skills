## 📌 Introduction

In an increasingly digitized world, users accumulate vast amounts of data daily, from personal photos and academic records to essential passwords. However, without systematic organization and cybersecurity awareness, critical information is easily lost. This activity focuses on creating a visually engaging, educational social media infographic titled **"DIGITAL FORGETFULNESS"** that highlights the risks of digital data loss and introduces an AI-powered organizational solution.

Through this project, I aimed to transform important information about digital organization, data preservation, and cybersecurity into an infographic that is easy to understand and visually engaging for social media audiences.

## 🎨 Design Choices

### 1. Color Palette & Block Grid Layout

- **Muted Tech Tones:** Uses a cohesive combination of steel blue, sage green, cool slate gray, and warm beige/gold backgrounds.
- **Section Grid:** Dividers split the content into distinct, numbered blocks (01 to 05), preventing visual clutter and establishing an easy-to-follow sequence.

### 2. Typography & Readability

- **Bold Primary Headers:** Sans-serif block typography for section titles (*THE PROBLEM*, *WHY IT MATTERS*, *THE SOLUTION*) ensures quick visual scanning.
- **Hierarchy:** Clear distinction between primary headers, underlined sub-headers (*Auto Organize*, *Smart Backup*, *Password Manager*), and supporting body text.

### 3. Visual Anchors & Vector Illustrations

- **Thematic Icons:** Incorporates relevant visual metaphors, such as a password padlock, 16GB flash drive, cyber threat icon, warning signal, and a mascot character, to keep the graphic lively and approachable.
- **Call-to-Action Footer:** Features a high-contrast banner at the bottom stating *"DON'T LET YOUR MEMORIES DISAPPEAR: Organize. Protect. Preserve."* paired with personal photo icons to create an emotional connection.

## 🛠️ Creative Process

### 1. Research & Information Architecture

Gathered facts on global data creation and security risks, structuring the narrative into a logical flow:

**Problem Identification (01–03) → Reality & Data Context (04) → Solution & Actionable Steps (05)**

This helped establish a clear sequence of information and ensured that the audience could understand the problem before being introduced to possible solutions.

### 2. Wireframing & Layout Structuring

Organized the content into a vertically scrolling 2-column grid system tailored for social media feeds (e.g., Pinterest, LinkedIn, or Instagram stories).

The layout was designed to make the information easier to follow while maintaining enough space for illustrations, headings, and supporting details.

### 3. Visual Assembly & Refining

Applied color-coded blocks to separate distinct ideas, added custom icons for rapid visual processing, and balanced text-heavy sections with clear whitespace.

I also considered the placement and size of the different elements to ensure that the infographic remained organized, readable, and visually engaging.

### 4. Academic Documentation & Citations

Integrated official cybersecurity and digital preservation references (NIST Framework & Digital Preservation Coalition) at the footer to support the information presented and maintain academic integrity.

The project documentation also served as a record of the infographic's concept, design decisions, and creative process.

## 📱 Purpose and Effectiveness of the Infographic

The infographic was designed to communicate the importance of organizing and protecting digital information in a way that social media users can easily understand.

By dividing the topic into numbered sections, I was able to present the problem, explain its importance, and introduce possible solutions in a logical order.

The combination of concise text, visual illustrations, color-coded sections, and a clear call to action helps make the information more accessible and encourages readers to become more mindful of their digital files and personal information.

Overall, the infographic demonstrates how visual communication can turn a technical topic into educational content that is easier to understand, remember, and apply in everyday digital activities.
