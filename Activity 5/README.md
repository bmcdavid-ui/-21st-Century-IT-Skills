## 📌 Introduction

For **Activity 5: AI Tools Activity**, I explored how artificial intelligence can assist in generating ideas, organizing information, and creating practical plans through the use of prompts. For this activity, I used a wedding planning scenario to compare the results of a generic prompt and a more detailed prompt.

The project focused on creating an intimate wedding plan for 20 guests in the Maldives, with a nature-inspired theme, an overlooking ocean view, disposable cameras, and giveaways connected to Maldivian culture and resources.

By comparing the two AI-generated outputs, I was able to observe how the amount of information and specificity included in a prompt can influence the relevance, creativity, and usefulness of the generated response.

> 💡 *"Better prompts lead to more relevant, personalized, and useful AI-generated results."*

## 🔍 Key Differences

**1. Detail and specificity**

Output 1 gave broad suggestions such as "multi-course dinner or private chef experience" and "welcome baskets, personalized gifts." Output 2 matched the brief with Handmade Lacquerware Boxes (*Liyelaa Jehun*), Pure Virgin Coconut Oil, and live Boduberu music during cocktail hour.

**2. Accuracy to prompt requirements**

Output 1 covered the general parameters (20 guests, scenic backdrop) but lacked destination-specific nuance. Output 2 addressed the requested parameters more directly:

| Requested | How Output 2 Delivered |
|:--|:--|
| 📍 Maldives | Maldives-inspired setting and overlooking deck setup |
| 👥 20 visitors | Guest count fixed at 20 throughout |
| 📷 Disposable cameras | Placement on seats, prompt cards, and return baskets |
| 🎁 Maldives giveaways | Items tied to Maldivian heritage and local natural resources |

**3. Clarity and structure**

Both outputs use tables, bold sections, and clear headings. Output 2 adds clarity by explaining how elements connect, such as pairing the timeline with film photography moments and custom favor displays.

**4. Practical usefulness**

Output 1 is a generic baseline that needs more research and tailoring. Output 2 is more personalized and detailed, making it a better starting point for vendor discussions, decor briefs, and favor sourcing.

## 🤖 Prompt Engineering and AI-Assisted Planning

This activity demonstrated how prompt engineering can influence the quality and relevance of AI-generated content.

### 1. Generic Prompting

The first prompt provided only a basic instruction. Because it lacked specific information, the AI had greater freedom to generate a general wedding plan.

### 2. Detailed Prompting

The second prompt included the destination, number of guests, desired atmosphere, photography activity, and giveaway preferences. These details helped guide the AI toward a more personalized and relevant response.

### 3. Importance of Clear Instructions

The comparison showed that providing context and specific requirements helps the AI better understand the intended outcome. Clear prompts can reduce unnecessary or unrelated suggestions and produce results that are more aligned with the user's needs.

### 4. Human Review and Improvement

Although AI can generate ideas and organize information quickly, its responses still require human evaluation. Details involving cultural traditions, local products, destination regulations, costs, and vendor availability should be verified before using an AI-generated plan in an actual event.

## 📝 Activity Reflection

### 📌 Activity Title and Description

**Activity 5: AI Tools Activity – Wedding Plan**

This activity focused on exploring the use of artificial intelligence in generating ideas and developing a wedding plan through prompt-based instructions. I created two different prompts: a generic prompt and a detailed prompt containing specific requirements for an intimate wedding in the Maldives. By comparing the generated outputs, I was able to understand how the information and instructions provided to an AI tool influence the quality, relevance, and level of detail of its responses.

### 💡 What Have I Learned?

Through this activity, I learned the importance of prompt engineering and how providing clear, specific, and organized instructions can improve AI-generated results. I gained a better understanding of how AI tools can assist in brainstorming, organizing ideas, creating plans, and generating suggestions based on given requirements. One challenge I encountered was making sure that the generated wedding plan followed all the details in the prompt while remaining organized, realistic, and relevant. I addressed this by providing more specific instructions in the second prompt and comparing the results to identify which details were properly incorporated. This activity also made me realize that AI-generated information should not always be accepted immediately, especially when it involves cultural details, actual costs, and practical arrangements that require further verification. In the future, I can apply these skills in academic activities, project planning, event organization, and other tasks that require research and idea development. I also want to improve my prompting skills by learning how to provide better context, establish clear limitations, and evaluate AI-generated responses more critically.
