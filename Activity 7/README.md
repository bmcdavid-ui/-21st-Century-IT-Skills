> ▌**"AI helps me draft and organize, while the accuracy and final decisions remain mine."**

**🌱 Seeing my growth.** Creating my resume with AI helped me see how much I have already done as a nursing student. Reading my clinical duties as one organized list, from scrubbing in major surgeries to assisting in Code Blue emergencies, made me prouder of my growth and showed me how my experiences fit together.

**⏱️ Speed is not judgment.** Both tools saved me time on formatting, which let me focus on reviewing the content. However, a quick draft is only useful if I read it carefully before using it.

**🔒 Privacy.** My prompt included my home address, phone number, and the contact details of my professors who serve as my references. Since they trusted me with their information, I should be careful about what I enter into online tools. Next time, I will use **placeholders** until my final version is ready.

**⚕️ Honesty in healthcare.** Wording such as "delegated responsibilities" can make a student sound like a licensed nurse. In healthcare, claiming more than I am allowed to do under supervision is a serious matter, so every word on my resume must match what I actually did.

**👀 Reading like an employer.** Comparing the two outputs pushed me to ask whether the key information was easy to find, whether the length was right, and whether the tone was confident without exaggerating. I now see AI as a collaborator, not a replacement for my own review.
