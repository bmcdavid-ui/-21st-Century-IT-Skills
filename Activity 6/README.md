> ▌**ABOUT THE PROJECT**

**DAGITAB Martial Arts Academy** is a proposed martial arts academy brand with a website designed to introduce its programs, communicate its values, and encourage people to begin their martial arts journey.

The website serves as an online platform where potential students and parents can explore the academy, learn about its training programs, view its gallery, and access enrollment and contact information.

The concept focuses on presenting martial arts as more than physical training. It also highlights the development of discipline, confidence, strength, and respect.

| **Website Type** | Martial Arts Academy Website |
| **Main Purpose** | Brand promotion, program information, and student inquiries |
| **Target Audience** | Children, teenagers, adults, and parents |
| **Main Programs** | Taekwondo, Kickboxing, and Kali-Arnis |
| **Visual Identity** | Bold, modern, and athletic |
| **Brand Colors** | Black, red, white, and gray |

### Intended Visitor Journey

The website's sections are arranged to guide potential students from discovering the academy to making an inquiry.

| Step | Visitor Action | Website Section |
|:-:|---|---|
| 01 | Discover the academy and its main message | Hero |
| 02 | Learn about the academy and its values | About |
| 03 | Explore the available martial arts programs | Programs |
| 04 | Understand the benefits of joining | Why DAGITAB |
| 05 | View training-related images | Gallery |
| 06 | Express interest through the enrollment form | Enrollment |
| 07 | Find contact and inquiry information | Contact & Footer |

### Main Website Features

- **Promotional Landing Page** – Introduces DAGITAB and presents its main message.
- **Program Information** – Provides descriptions of the three martial arts programs.
- **Visual Gallery** – Presents images that help visitors experience the academy's training environment.
- **Enrollment Section** – Provides a form for individuals interested in joining.
- **Call-to-Action Buttons** – Direct visitors toward exploring programs or enrolling.
- **Contact Information** – Provides ways for visitors to reach the academy.

> ▌**"A good website should not only look appealing, but also serve a purpose."**

This activity gave me a better understanding of how websites are created and how important they are in promoting a business or organization. I learned how to use AI-powered website-building tools to turn an idea into an actual website for **DAGITAB Martial Arts Academy**. It also helped me understand that a website should not only focus on its appearance but should also provide useful information and make it easier for visitors to take action.

One of the things I learned was the importance of writing **clear and detailed prompts**. The instructions given to an AI tool can affect the website's layout, content, colors, and overall appearance. I also realized that planning the website's sections beforehand helps organize the information and makes the visitor's experience more convenient.

Another important part of this activity was translating the academy's identity into a consistent website design. I used a **red-and-black theme** because these colors give DAGITAB a bold identity that represents strength, discipline, and confidence. I also learned how the proper use of images, typography, buttons, and content can make a website more attractive and easier to navigate.

I also realized that creating a website involves more than generating its design. Its content, links, forms, and other features should be reviewed and tested to make sure they work as intended. If I were to improve this project further, I would focus on testing its functions, improving its mobile responsiveness, and making the enrollment process more convenient for potential students.

Overall, this activity improved my **creativity, problem-solving skills, and digital literacy**. It showed me how AI can help turn ideas into practical outputs, but it also reminded me that human input, careful planning, and evaluation are still important in producing a useful website. I believe the skills I learned from this activity will be helpful in future academic projects and other digital activities.
